# Empirical data sources

The package provides source links for the paper's empirical applications.
It does not include empirical datasets or empirical-analysis code.

| Data | Source |
|---|---|
| Chinese stock returns, market capitalization, and three-factor data | [CSMAR](https://data.csmar.com/) |
| U.S. adjusted stock-price archive | [Kaggle: S&P 500 Stocks — 25 Years of Data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/darkmatternet/s-and-p-500-stocks-25-years-of-data-updated-daily) |
| U.S. point-in-time constituent stock-price archive | [Kaggle: S&P500 index stocks](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/joebeachcapital/s-and-p500-index-stocks-daily-updated) |
| Historical S&P 500 membership | [fja05680/sp500](https://github.com/fja05680/sp500) |
| U.S. Fama–French three-factor and five-factor series, including risk-free rates | [Kenneth R. French Data Library](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html) |
| Public Chinese deposit-rate history used for the risk-free benchmark | [Fujian Rural Credit](https://www.fjnx.com.cn/xpls/dtjrxx/rmbckllb/index.html), [Postal Savings Bank disclosure](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H2_AN201906281336805446_1.pdf), and [Beijing government rate-change notice](https://www.beijing.gov.cn/fwcj/jiage/zffw1/65dc557711a82834a863972e.html) |

CSMAR requires appropriate institutional or other authorized database access;
see its [official access guide](https://www.csmar.com/contents/9/861.html).
Kaggle may require an account. Each source's access and usage terms apply.
The U.S. price and membership archives are third-party sources, not CRSP.
Public datasets can be revised, so later downloads may differ from the versions
used in the paper.
